Predictive Modeling of Chlorine Decay & Disinfection Byproducts using PINN

Implemented a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) architecture in Python and PyTorch to solve inverse problems for reactive transport and chemical kinetic parameters in water distribution networks. Embedded governing differential equations directly into the loss objective to enforce physical mass-conservation constraints under sparse and noisy boundary data.

B–B

UR5 Manipulator Forward Dynamics Simulation Engine

Formulated equations of motion for a 6-DOF UR5 manipulator using the Recursive Newton-Euler Algorithm (RNEA) and Lie group formulations (SE(3)) to eliminate geometric representation singularities. Derived spatial inertia matrices, joint twists, and dynamic equations of motion, coupled with CoppeliaSim physics simulations to validate torque-driven trajectory execution.
